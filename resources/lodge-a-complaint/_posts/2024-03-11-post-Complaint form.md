<p><strong>To file a complaint against a psychiatrist who is a member of the Panel of Psychiatrists &lt;embed link to Register&gt;:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>If you are a Singpass user, please use this form &lt;to link&gt;.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You may use this form &lt;to link&gt; if you do not have Singpass.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>You are strongly encouraged to use the Singpass-enabled form if you are
a Singpass user, as it enables the Selection Committee to verify your identity
and process your complaint more expeditiously.</p>
<p><strong>Before you submit your complaint, please note that:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The Panel was established to safeguard the objectivity and quality of
psychiatric expert evidence. Only psychiatrists who are admitted to the
Panel may give evidence on a matter concerning psychiatry in criminal proceedings
in Singapore.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Your complaint should be limited to complaints about a <a href="/how-to-apply/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">psychiatrist's eligibility to remain on the Panel </a>and
to give expert evidence in criminal proceedings in Singapore.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You will be required to upload a statutory declaration that is made in
accordance with the First Schedule of the Oaths and Declarations Act 2000.
If you do not have the necessary statutory declaration, please obtain one
before submitting your complaint.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>After the Selection Committee considers your complaint, the Selection
Committee may (a) start revocation proceedings against the psychiatrist;
or (b) dismiss the complaint. You will be notified of the Selection Committee’s
decision.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
